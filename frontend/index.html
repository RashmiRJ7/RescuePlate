How it works

Three simple steps to rescue a meal.

From a hostel mess in Pune to a wedding hall in Delhi — surplus food finds its way to people who need it.

Step 01

List surplus food

Donors post what’s available — 40 plates of dal-rice, 25 bread packets, or 120 wedding portions — with a pickup window.

Step 02

NGOs discover nearby

Nearby organisations see fresh listings, check quantity and safe-until time, and claim what they can collect.

Step 03

Food gets rescued

The NGO collects in time, serves the meal, and the rescue is confirmed. Good food reaches people, not landfills.
