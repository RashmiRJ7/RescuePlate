How RescuePlate works

Three steps from surplus to a served meal.

Donors list what they have. NGOs claim what they can collect. The handoff happens before the food expires.

Step 01

Donate Food

Restaurants, caterers, and households post surplus with quantity, location, and the time it must be collected.

Step 02

Find NGO

Nearby organisations see open listings that match their capacity and can respond while the food is still safe to eat.

Step 03

Rescue Food

An NGO claims the listing, collects it in the pickup window, and confirms the rescue so nothing edible is left waiting.
